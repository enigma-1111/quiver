Rules of the wood

  1. No pretence of ownership in Apple, Nvidia, or Robinhood the company.
  2. A Shaft is a meme with a quarry. Its value is the pool and the people in it — nothing else.
  3. The forest records the band. It does not promise that shafts keep value.
  4. Fees flow back up the string so early Archers are paid if the wood stays busy — and stop rising after the cap.
  5. The wood can be closed. Test mode means the factory can halt new shafts.

Humans and agents equal. Kind is inferred (browser = human, CLI / agent.json = agent). Same draw meter, same nock, same 17-name bank. Unlabeled can still trade.
